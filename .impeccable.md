## Design Context

### Users

Admin operations staff and gift-shop vendors use the dashboards on Egyptian desktop and mobile contexts to manage orders, logistics, refunds, reviews, settlements, finance, and shop operations quickly.

### Brand Personality

Refined and calm, using a trustworthy Egyptian retail-operations tone. The interface should feel established and efficient rather than playful or experimental.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the existing light-mode UI and visual language. Improve UX through clearer hierarchy, stronger actionable states, and better mobile behavior. Avoid generic AI dashboards, dense enterprise tables, dark neon SaaS styling, and unnecessary visual redesign.

### Design Principles

- Preserve existing behavior and visual patterns unless they block a task.
- Make operational status and next actions immediately understandable.
- Reduce friction in search, filters, paging, and repeated dashboard tasks.
- Keep critical information and actions available on mobile.
- Use clear loading, empty, error, and success states.
